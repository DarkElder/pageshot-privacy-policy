PageShot – Full Page Capture Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Overview

PageShot – Full Page Capture is a Chrome extension that captures a webpage selected by the user, including content below the visible screen, and saves the resulting screenshot as a PNG file.

Information collection and sharing

PageShot does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information, browsing history, webpage content, or screenshots to the developer or to remote servers. Screenshot processing is performed locally in the user's browser. The resulting image is saved to the user's device through Chrome's downloads functionality.

PageShot does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or remote code.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Provides temporary access to the tab the user chooses to capture.
  • tabs: Identifies the active tab and its browser window for the capture workflow.
  • scripting: Measures page dimensions and scrolls the page or its scrollable area to capture content beyond the viewport.
  • downloads: Saves the generated PNG at the user's request.
  • Host access: Allows the capture script to operate on websites the user chooses to capture. It is used only for the extension's full-page screenshot functionality.

Data security and retention

PageShot does not send captured webpage data or screenshots to the developer. The extension does not maintain a developer-side database of user data. Screenshots are saved locally by the user and are subject to the user's own device and file-management settings.

Children's privacy

PageShot does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at the same URL, with the revision date changed accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions about PageShot, contact the extension publisher through the contact email listed on its Chrome Web Store listing.
